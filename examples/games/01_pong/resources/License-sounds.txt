hit.wav, point.wav and win.wav

Synthesized for this repository by tools/make_example_art.py (pong_sounds): square waves at 22050 Hz, 16-bit mono, with a
2 ms attack and a quadratic decay. hit is one 480 Hz blip of 60 ms; point is

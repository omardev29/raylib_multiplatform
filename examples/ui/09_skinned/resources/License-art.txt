skin.aseprite, spinner.aseprite, click.wav, hover.wav and nope.wav

Drawn and synthesized for this repository by tools/make_skinned_art.py: the
skin's frames, panel, icons and bar with Pillow's shapes, the spinner as four
frames of a turning star, and the sounds as square waves at 22050 Hz, 16-bit
mono, with a 2 ms attack and a quadratic decay (click 720 Hz for 50 ms, hover
1200 Hz for 20 ms, nope 220 Hz then 160 Hz, 80 ms each).
